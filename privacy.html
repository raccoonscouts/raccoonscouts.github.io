Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026. This policy applies to the website raccoonscouts.github.io, to the Raccoon Scouts social media accounts and to the "Raccoon Scouts Reels" application. Contact: raccoonscouts@gmail.com.

1. What the application does

"Raccoon Scouts Reels" is an internal tool used only by the Raccoon Scouts team to upload short videos that we create ourselves to our own accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads). It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's accounts.

2. Data we access through platform APIs

  • Only data of our own accounts that is needed to publish videos: account/channel identifier and name, upload status of our videos.
  • Authorization tokens are stored on our own server, are never shared with third parties and are used only to upload our videos.
  • We do not access, collect or store data of other users through these APIs.

3. YouTube API Services

The application uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with our YouTube content you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the application can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

4. Messages you send us

If you write to our accounts in direct messages, we use your messages and public profile name only to answer you and to connect you with a curator of the team. We do not sell or share this information with third parties for advertising. You can ask us to delete your conversation at any time by writing to the same account or to raccoonscouts@gmail.com; we delete it within 30 days.

5. This website

The website does not use cookies, analytics or forms and does not collect personal data. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request data (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy statement.

6. Age

Our offers are intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly process data of anyone under 18.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
